# Music: Recall launch film

## What's in the renders now

Every cut ships with an **original score, synthesised in code** (`scripts/score.mjs`): no samples and no third-party music, so there's nothing to license. It follows the structure below. Every hit is placed from the same timing data as the picture, so it lands on the frame. Measured onsets are within 5 ms of their picture events.
- glass tocks for each absorption;
- key clicks for each keystroke;
- a held note when ⌥Space stops time;
- a bowed reach, a thud and a sub drop for the privacy beat;
- a light groove under the numbers;
- a resolve on the icon.

All cuts are loudness-normalised to -16 LUFS integrated, with true peak below -1.5 dBTP.

It is a good synthesised score, not a composer's. For a bigger launch, commission or license a track to the brief below and drop it in. `out/score-*.wav` shows what each section is doing, and `out/click-*.wav` is the beat grid with a ping on every hit point.

The film is cut to a fixed grid: 100 BPM, set in `src/constants.ts`. If the music changes tempo, change `BPM` and every cut moves with it. Pick a BPM where one beat is a whole number of frames at 60 fps: 90, 100, 120 or 150.

## What to license or commission (optional upgrade)

**Tempo and form.** 100 BPM, 4/4, 24 bars for the 57.6 s master, ending on bar 25 beat 1 (silence). It needs a clean edit to a 38-beat (22.8 s) version for the 16:9 short, a 33-beat (19.8 s) version for the 9:16 cut and a 50-beat (30 s) version for the 1:1 cut. Stems help: pad, pulse, percussion and motif.

**Mood.** Patient, warm, a little uncanny at first, then quietly confident. Close-mic'd and intimate, not epic. It should feel like the inside of a thought, not a trailer. Major key overall, with a suspended or unresolved colour during the "three weeks later" section that resolves only on the ending.

**Palette.**
- A soft analogue or felt-piano pad.
- Glass or kalimba "tocks" for the five absorptions, each a step higher.
- A dry tick for results.
- A low bowed or sine tone that stretches for the privacy reach.
- Light, dry percussion (rim, shaker, soft kick) only in the numbers section.
- A three-note motif that first appears under "It finds what you meant" and returns resolved on the icon.

**Structure.**

| Section | Bars | Music |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Absorb | 1–5 | pad, no pulse; tocks on each absorption; felt pulse from bar 4 |
| Tip of the tongue | 6–8 | thin and suspended; one wavering note |
| ⌥Space | 9.1 | **everything stops except one held high note**: the pause in time |
| Panel | 9–13 | sparse; ticks when results land |
| Meaning | 14–15 | the motif enters |
| Surface tension | 16–18 | drone, a rising strain, then a soft release |
| Measured | 19–21 | the first full groove, light and dry |
| Resolve | 22–25 | the groove drops out; the root chord resolves under the icon; ring out |

**Hard rules.**
- No whoosh on every cut. No risers into the logo. No vocal.
- The silence or held note on ⌥Space is the most important sound in the film. Protect it.
- The wordmark lands on a downbeat (bar 23.1, 00:52:48).

**References** (for feel, not to imitate):
- the restraint of Apple's quieter product films, such as the "Shot on iPhone" ambient spots;
- Ólafur Arnalds' felt-piano textures;
- Nils Frahm's *Says* for patience before a release.

Any track should be cleared for worldwide online advertising use, perpetual, including paid social.

**Voice.** None is needed: the type carries the film. If you add a voice-over, keep it to the on-screen lines and record it dry.

## Cutdowns

The short, 9:16 and 1:1 click tracks ping at each scene start instead of the cues above. Scene starts are in `SHORT`, `VERTICAL` and `SQUARE` in `src/constants.ts`, and each cut's score is built from them in `scripts/score.mjs`.
